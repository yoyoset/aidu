### [简体中文] - 商店说明

**让 AI 成为你的私人外教，开启“降维打击”式的英语精读体验。**

AIDU (Adaptive Immersive Deep English) 是一款专为深度学习者和学生设计的 Chrome 浏览器插件。它不只是一个翻译器，而是一个驻扎在网页侧边栏的 **AI 私人导师**，致力于帮你从“查单词”跨越到“懂语境”。

#### 🌟 核心功能

* **🤖 AI 私人外教 (Persona-based Explanations)**
    AI 会化身不同身份为你讲解。特别是**“小学老师”模式**，会用极度简单、亲切的语言分解复杂的长难句，对**小朋友和零基础学习者**极其友好。

* **🧩 智能分词与精读 (Precision Tokenization)**
    不再是生硬的全文翻译。AIDU 采用先进的语义分词技术，将复杂的英语句子拆解为易于理解的语块，并自动捕捉标准翻译软件容易忽略的“动词短语”和“熟词僻义”。

* **📖 深度阅读体验 (Deep Reading)**
    点击网页上的任何单词，即可在侧边栏获得深度的语法、语境分析。所有生词会自动关联阅读时的原句，让你在鲜活的语境中记忆，彻底告别孤立背单词。

* **🪄 AI 魔法生词本**
    内置最新的 FSRS 间隔复习算法。AI 还能为你生成专属的创意例句，确保每一个记下的单词都能真正内化为你的知识。

#### 🛡️ 隐私与安全

* **本地优先**：数据存储在你的浏览器，未授权绝不外泄。
* **小朋友友好**：界面纯净无广告，专注学习本身。
* **自由支配**：支持 GitHub Gist 或 Cloudflare 自定义同步，你的数据你做主。

**立即安装 AIDU，让每一篇网页文章都变成你的专属英语私教课！**

### [English] - Store Description

**Meet your personal AI Language Tutor. Master Deep Reading with "Context-Aware" Intelligence.**

AIDU (Adaptive Immersive Deep English) is a revolutionary Chrome extension designed to bridge the gap between superficial translation and lifelong language acquisition. It’s not just a tool; it’s an **AI-powered Reading Coach** that lives in your browser sidepanel.

#### 🌟 Key Features

* **🤖 AI Private Tutor (Dynamic Personas)**
    AI adapts its teaching style to your level. Use the **"Primary School Teacher" mode** for crystal-clear, friendly explanations of complex grammar – making it **exceptionally kid-friendly** and accessible for beginners.

* **🧩 Smart Segmentation & Tokenization**
    Go beyond word-for-word translation. AIDU breaks down long sentences into structured semantic blocks, automatically flagging phrasal verbs and "hidden meanings" that standard translators miss.

* **📖 Immersive Deep Reading**
    Click any word to unlock deep context analysis. Every word you save is permanently linked to the **original reading sentence**, ensuring you learn through context, not isolation.

* **🪄 Magic Vocabulary Builder (Spaced Repetition)**
    Powered by the cutting-edge **FSRS algorithm**, AIDU optimizes your review schedule based on real memory science. Let the AI generate creative, relevant example sentences for any word you capture.

#### 🛡️ Privacy & Reliability

* **Local-First Architecture**: Your learning history stays in your browser.
* **Safe for Kids**: Ad-free, clean UI focused entirely on education.
* **Own Your Data**: Decentralized sync via GitHub Gist or Cloudflare Workers.

**Install AIDU today and turn any website into a high-end, personalized English classroom!**
