# Privacy Policy for AIDU - English Learning Assistant

**Effective Date:** February 11, 2026

At **SquareUncle (方砖叔)**, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **AIDU** (the "Extension") handles your information.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

AIDU is designed with a **"Local-First"** philosophy.

- **Personal Information:** The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers.
- **Reading Data:** All text you select for analysis or save to your library is stored locally within your browser's storage.
- **Vocabulary & Progress:** Your learning progress and vocabulary lists are stored on your device.

## 2. Third-Party API Integration

To provide intelligent analysis, AIDU allows you to connect to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) providers (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or DeepSeek).

- When you request an analysis, the strictly necessary text and context are sent directly to the API provider you have configured.
- We do not intercept or store these API calls. Your interactions with these providers are governed by their respective privacy policies.

## 3. Storage and Sync

- **Local Storage:** Data is saved using Chrome's `storage.local` and `unlimitedStorage` APIs.
- **Optional Cloud Sync:** If you choose to enable synchronization (via GitHub Gist or Cloudflare Workers), your data will be encrypted/transmitted to your own private storage. We do not have access to your sync credentials or data.

## 4. Permissions Justification

- `storage` & `unlimitedStorage`: To save your reading lists and vocabulary locally.
- `sidePanel`: To provide an immersive reading and learning interface.
- `activeTab` & `scripting`: To allow you to save articles and interact with the text you are reading.
- `contextMenus`: To provide the "Save to AIDU" right-click option.
- `host_permissions` (<all_urls>): Required to allow the Extension to function on any educational website or article you choose to read.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top.

## 6. Contact
